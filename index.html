1. Overview

House Of Java (HOJ) is an event, attendance, notification, profile, and administrative application. Depending on your role and the features enabled by the organization operating the HOJ server, the app may process account information, attendance records, event data, device identifiers, precise location, QR codes, profile images, and finance-related records.

HOJ is designed to communicate with an HOJ server selected by the user or organization. In many deployments, that server is operated locally by the school, organization, event administrator, or other entity using HOJ.

3. Precise location data

HOJ may request precise location permission when a student generates an attendance QR code. Location is used to compare the student's current position with the coordinates and attendance radius configured for the event.

The app may send the current latitude, longitude, and reported location accuracy to the connected HOJ server for attendance validation. HOJ does not need continuous background location tracking for its normal attendance flow.

If precise location permission is denied, location services are disabled, or the location reading is not sufficiently accurate, location-based attendance QR generation may be unavailable.

4. Camera access and QR codes

HOJ uses camera permission for QR-code scanning. This includes scanning a server connection QR code and, for authorized administrators, scanning student attendance QR codes.

Camera frames are used for QR recognition. HOJ does not require users to take or upload a photograph simply to scan a QR code.

5. Device information

HOJ may use an Android device identifier to associate a student account with a registered device. This is intended to reduce unauthorized attendance activity. If a user changes devices, an administrator may need to approve a device-regeneration or device-change request.

6. How information is used

Information handled through HOJ may be used to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Apply Student and Administrator access permissions.
  • Display and manage events.
  • Generate, scan, validate, and confirm attendance QR codes.
  • Verify attendance location and enforce configured event-radius rules.
  • Maintain attendance records and points.
  • Send or display app notifications and announcements.
  • Manage approved device changes.
  • Store profile, event, and optional finance-related images.
  • Support administrative finance records and synchronization.
  • Protect the service against misuse and investigate technical problems.

7. Data storage and HOJ servers

HOJ is designed to connect to an HOJ server. Information submitted through the app may be stored in the database and file storage configured by the organization operating that server. The organization may operate the server on a local network, private computer, hosted server, or other infrastructure.

Some app data may also be stored locally on the Android device, including session information, selected server settings, and offline administrative finance records. Authentication credentials and tokens should be protected using the security facilities provided by Android.

Because HOJ can be self-hosted, the operator of the server is responsible for the server's hosting environment, database access controls, backups, network security, and applicable organizational data-handling practices.

8. Sharing and disclosure

HOJ does not require the sale of personal information. Information may be accessible to authorized administrators and to the organization operating the HOJ server where necessary to provide the application's functions.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • Protect users, administrators, the organization, or the security of the service.
  • Investigate abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access.
  • Support infrastructure providers used by the organization operating the server, where applicable.

9. Data retention

Data may be retained for as long as necessary to provide HOJ functions, maintain legitimate attendance and administrative records, meet organizational requirements, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Certain short-lived technical records, such as rotating attendance QR tokens and expired authentication tokens, may be automatically invalidated or removed according to server settings. Retention periods for account, attendance, event, finance, and audit information may depend on the organization operating the HOJ server.

10. Security

HOJ uses reasonable technical safeguards appropriate to its architecture, including authenticated API access, role-based authorization, password hashing, token-based sessions, rotating attendance QR tokens, device registration, and server-side attendance validation.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Organizations deploying HOJ are responsible for securely configuring their server, network, database, administrative accounts, backups, and transport security.

11. Your choices and permissions

Android allows you to manage permissions such as Camera, Precise Location, and Notifications through your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

Depending on your role and the policies of the organization operating your HOJ server, you may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

12. Account deletion

You may request deletion of your HOJ account and associated personal information by contacting the organization or administrator responsible for your HOJ server, or by using the contact method listed below.

Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate organizational, attendance, financial, security, or legal purposes. Where retention is required, access should be limited to authorized personnel and the information should not be used for unrelated purposes.

13. Students and minors

HOJ may be deployed by educational or student organizations. Where users are minors, the organization operating HOJ is responsible for determining and obtaining any consent or authorization required by applicable law or institutional policy.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when HOJ features, data practices, legal requirements, or deployment arrangements change. The updated version will display a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes should be communicated through an appropriate channel when required.
